Kosher dairy, under the Vaad of Flatbush

The store kitchen is dairy only, so nothing here contains meat. All cheese is halav yisrael. Meat dishes are made for catering orders in a separate kitchen.

The person behind the food.

JuliePortrait coming for the demo

Julie's Kitchen started with platters and boards for friends and family, and grew into boutique catering across the tri-state area.

Today it is two things under one roof: a kosher dairy market and café at 514 Kings Highway, and a catering kitchen cooking for Shabbat, holidays, brunches and celebrations of every size. Julie also teaches private and group cooking lessons for adults and children.

Her recipe book, Julie's Holiday Table, collects more than 30 tried and true holiday recipes.
